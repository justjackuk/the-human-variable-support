Player support

Keep your story moving.

Support is available in English. For help with The Human Variable, email jack@byteandtide.co.uk.

What to include

Tell us your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, the scenario or campaign name, and what happened. A screenshot can help. Do not send passwords, verification codes, payment-card details or your complete decision history unless it is needed and you choose to share it.

What does annual access include?

Annual access includes all 100 standalone scenarios, every campaign and all preparation cards while your subscription is active. Subscribers do not pay extra for individual stories or the campaign bundle. The proposed UK price is £4.99 per year; Apple shows the applicable local price and renewal terms before purchase.

Can I play without a subscription?

The Public Ledger, the first standalone scenario, is free. Optional individual scenario purchases permanently unlock that story. The Public Life Collection is a separate permanent bundle containing three campaigns: Contingency: Three Shifts, Common Ground and On the Record. The UK prices are 99p per individual scenario and £1.99 for the three-campaign bundle, subject to the price shown by Apple. Permanent purchases remain subject to refunds and revocations.

I purchased access but a story is locked.

Open the story’s access screen and select Restore purchases. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase and allow an internet connection for Apple to refresh access. Check the subscription’s paid period in Apple’s subscription settings. If the problem continues, contact support with the product name and a description of the problem; do not send payment-card information.

Can I play offline?

Stories and decision records are stored on your device. After access has been verified, the game checks the verified entitlement and its expiry locally. Buying, restoring or refreshing purchases requires an internet connection to Apple. A subscription cannot unlock play after its verified paid period ends.

Can I undo or restart a decision?

Committed decisions persist. The game may offer a later change of course, but it does not erase the earlier commitment. Preparation cards open specific authored options and have defined costs; they do not guarantee a successful outcome or rewrite history.

My saved record cannot be read.

The game pauses rather than replacing the latest record with an older one. Close the story and contact support. If saving failed, check available device storage. Avoid uninstalling the app while seeking help: uninstalling can remove local records. This version has no account-based cloud recovery service.

How do I cancel, restore or request a refund?

Manage renewal through Apple subscriptions. Cancelling stops future renewal; entitled access continues until the paid period ends. Saved journals remain readable afterwards. Use Restore purchases in the app to refresh entitlements. Apple handles refund requests through Report a Problem.

Is this government advice or a personality test?

No. Institutions, characters and outcomes are fictional. The game does not provide government, legal, medical or financial advice and does not score your personality or political identity. It is intended for adults aged 18 and over and includes moral pressure, conflict, fictional disaster and injury without graphic imagery.

Privacy policy

Your decisions stay in your game.

Effective 9 November 2026 · Applies to The Human Variable version 1.0 and this support website.

Who is responsible

The Human Variable is developed by Jack Bowden trading as Byte & Tide. Contact jack@byteandtide.co.uk for privacy questions or requests.

Local game records

The app stores scenario and campaign decisions, preparation-card use, progress and adult self-attestation in local files on your device. It does not transmit these records to Byte & Tide. There is no game account, advertising tracker, analytics SDK or connected narrative AI service in this version. The game does not request your political affiliation or create a personality profile.

Snapshots are retained so earlier commitments remain part of the record. They remain while the app’s data is retained. Your normal device backups may include these files under Apple’s backup settings. Uninstalling the app can remove local data. This version has no in-app reset or Byte & Tide cloud restore service. Local files are not separately encrypted by the app; device security and backups are managed by iOS and your settings.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes payments and manages your Apple Account’s transactions. The app reads verified product identifiers, transaction and entitlement information, subscription expiry and revocation status to determine access. Byte & Tide does not receive your payment-card details. Purchase access can be refreshed through Restore purchases. Apple’s own privacy practices apply to its services: Apple Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics and TestFlight

The app contains no custom diagnostics-upload service. Apple may process usage, crash or TestFlight feedback information under its services and your device or testing settings, and may make relevant reports available to the developer. If you send feedback or a screenshot through TestFlight, review it before sending because it may show your game decisions.

If you contact support

We receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond, investigate problems and keep a record of the support exchange. Our basis for this processing is our legitimate interest in providing support and maintaining the app, or taking steps to provide the service you request. Support correspondence is kept only as needed to handle the issue, related follow-up and applicable legal obligations. Do not include sensitive personal information unnecessarily. Email service providers process correspondence to deliver and store it.

This website

This static support site does not include an analytics service, advertising code, contact form or cookies set by Byte & Tide. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider processes technical information needed to serve and secure requests, such as IP addresses and browser/request information, under its own GitHub Privacy Statement. Following an external link takes you to that provider’s service and privacy practices.

Your choices and rights

You control device backups, Apple subscriptions and information you send to support. Byte & Tide cannot retrieve or remotely delete game records it does not receive. For personal information held in support correspondence, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or object to processing where applicable. Contact the address above; we may need proportionate information to verify a request. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.

Adults and policy changes

The game is intended for adults aged 18 and over. Its in-app age confirmation is self-attestation. If our data practices change, we will update this policy and the relevant app disclosures. The effective date above identifies this version.

Terms of use

Clear access. Persistent choices.

The app uses Apple’s Standard End User Licence Agreement. Apple presents the applicable price, payment and subscription terms before confirmation.

Annual access automatically renews each year unless cancelled through Apple. Cancelling renewal does not erase saved records.
